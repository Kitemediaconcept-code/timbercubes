Can I order a wardrobe and a cot together as one bedroom project?

Yes. Ordering everything from one maker keeps finishes matched and sizes in proportion. As bedroom interior manufacturers Thrissur, we give one quote, one timeline and one team for the whole room. A single wardrobe or cot can also be ordered on its own.

Which materials suit Kerala’s humidity for bedroom furniture?

Kerala’s monsoon humidity can swell weak boards and loosen fittings. For bedroom interior design in Kerala, options include WPC, PVC foam board, BWP 710 Gurjan marine plywood and HDHMR, chosen by each wall’s moisture exposure and the expected lifespan. Each option and its cost is explained at the site visit.

How long does a bedroom wardrobe take?

Most bedroom wardrobes take 30 to 35 days. Larger designs, special finishes or an unfinished site can add time. The schedule is confirmed before production, so painting, flooring or house shifting can be planned around the installation date.

Can you plan a wardrobe for a compact bedroom?

Yes. A sliding door wardrobe needs no swing space, which suits compact flats and small bedrooms. Loft storage above uses the wall up to the ceiling. Each wardrobe is planned from the room’s actual measurements, including window, door and switch positions.

What warranty do bedroom interiors come with?

Warranty ranges from 6 months to 25 years, depending on the board, finish and hardware chosen. Longer cover applies to selected materials, and shorter cover to some fittings. The exact terms for the wardrobe, cot and side tables are listed in the quotation before work begins.
