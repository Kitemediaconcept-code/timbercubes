Ask Us on WhatsApp
Can the furniture be made to custom sizes?

Yes. Every TV unit, console and workstation is made to the measurements taken on site, and a custom sofa Thrissur can be planned around the room.

Which areas do you install in?

Living room interiors are installed across Thrissur, Malappuram, Ernakulam, Kozhikode (Calicut) and Palakkad. For any other location, call 08129 188 188 to check whether installation is possible.

How long does a living room interior take?

Most living room units take 25 to 30 days to make and install. The final timeline depends on the materials, the level of customisation and site readiness.

Can the design work within my budget?

Yes, different materials, finishes and hardware can be suggested to balance looks, durability and cost. The quote is prepared only after the room is measured on site.

Is there a 3D preview of the living room design?

Yes, 3D visuals can be prepared to show the layout, colours and finishes before production. 3D design is a chargeable service, and charges are shared in advance.
