A Fresh Start for Timbercubes: Our New Brand Identity and Logo

25 January 2017 was a special day for Timbercubes, as we unveiled our new brand identity and logo. The launch took place at The Mercy Luxury Business Hotel in Kochi, where we shared the moment with our guests.

Our Story Since 2008: What We Do at Timbercubes

Our story began in 2008. Since then, we have worked on homes and commercial spaces across Kerala, shaping each one to be practical, comfortable and pleasing to the eye.

We make modular kitchens, custom wardrobes and complete interiors. Some customers come to us with one interior requirement, while others want a full home or office done. Either way, we start by listening to what the customer needs. With that in mind, we plan the space, choose the right materials and finish the work with care, so the result suits their lifestyle, taste and purpose.

Why We Were Ready for a New Identity

By 2017, we had nine years of experience, many completed projects and strong relationships with our customers. All of this gave us the confidence to think bigger. A new identity and logo became our way of showing that we were ready for the next stage of growth.

The Guests Who Honoured Our Launch

It meant a lot to have these guests with us at the launch:

  • Mr. Kochouseph Chittilappilly, Founder & Chairman, V-Guard Industries Ltd.
  • Mr. T. S. Pattabhiraman, Chairman & Managing Director, Kalyan Silks
  • Mr. Sathya Narayanan, Varma & Varma Chartered Accountants
  • Mr. Shameem Rafeeq, Spark Stories

We are grateful to each of them for being a part of this special day.

What Our New Identity Means to Us

The new look was about more than a logo. It showed our growing abilities and our renewed vision. Along with it came our commitment to offer better products, fresh ideas and improved service as the world of interiors keeps changing.

The Nine Years That Shaped Timbercubes

Those nine years taught us a lot. Designing and building interiors was only one part of the journey. Along the way, we learned to understand people, to create spaces that make daily life easier, to build lasting relationships and to earn our customers' trust.

The new identity marked the start of the next stage in that journey. We are thankful to everyone who has walked with us so far.

Founder & Blog Author

Abdul Jalal Panthenkadan

Founder & Blog Author, Timbercubes (Est. 2008)

~20 Years in Interior Industry 8 Years UAE Business Experience NIPHM Certified in Pest Management

Abdul Jalal Panthenkadan is the founder of Timbercubes, established in 2008. He has nearly two decades of experience in the interior industry, and eight years of professional experience in Dubai, UAE, gave him a broader understanding of business.

His expertise covers modern interior concepts, kitchen and wardrobe manufacturing, furniture materials, hardware and emerging design trends. He has also completed a certification course in Rodent and Household Pest Management from the National Institute of Plant Health Management (NIPHM), Government of India, which adds to his knowledge of protecting interiors and materials.

Through his blogs and articles, he shares his industry knowledge, practical experience and views on the latest developments in interior design and manufacturing. His aim is to give readers useful ideas that help them understand interior spaces, materials and design possibilities.

He believes good interiors are not just about appearance. They should make spaces beautiful, functional and comfortable, and this belief guides his work at Timbercubes.
