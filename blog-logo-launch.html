A Milestone in Our Journey: The Logo Launch

A New Identity. The Same Commitment.

On 25 January 2017, Timbercubes hosted a landmark gathering at The Mercy, Kochi, celebrating the official unveiling of our refined brand identity and modern corporate emblem. The launch stood as a pivotal moment honoring our roots while articulating our future vision for high-precision bespoke modular interiors across Kerala.

The newly unveiled identity symbolizes the core philosophies of Timbercubes: geometric symmetry, architectural elegance, and timeless durability. Guests, architects, valued clients, and company pioneers gathered together to inaugurate the visual transformation.

Key Highlights of the Event

  • Official Logo Unveiling: The ceremonial launch of our signature cubic emblem reflecting precision woodwork and engineering.
  • Architect & Partner Meet: Productive interactions with South India's premier interior architects on evolving modular kitchen trends.
  • Future Roadmap: Expanding manufacturing capacity and introducing 25-year structural warranties on specialized cabinetry.

As we continue expanding our footprint across Kerala, this milestone remains a foundational memory of our dedication to craftsmanship, precision, and heartfelt service to each family home we shape.
