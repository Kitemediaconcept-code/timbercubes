What types of commercial spaces does Timbercubes handle?

Offices, corporate workplaces, shops, showrooms, clinics, restaurants, cafés and educational spaces. A project can be a single reception area or a complete fit-out.

Is the furniture manufactured by Timbercubes?

Yes. Workstations, counters, display units and storage are made at Timbercubes’ own factory in Kerala. Each unit is checked for size, finish and hardware fitting before dispatch.

Can you handle a complete commercial interior project?

Yes. Furniture, wall panelling, false ceilings, partitions and painting can all be handled by one team, from design and material selection to manufacturing, installation and finishing.

How is the quotation prepared?

The team visits the site, takes measurements and discusses requirements. The quotation lists the space size, design, materials, finishes, hardware and scope of work, with clear inclusions and exclusions.

Which areas around Thrissur do you serve?

Timbercubes serves Kunnamkulam, Guruvayur, Chalakudy, Irinjalakuda, Kodungallur and other parts of Thrissur, along with Malappuram, Palakkad, Ernakulam and Kozhikode. Larger projects elsewhere in Kerala can be discussed.
