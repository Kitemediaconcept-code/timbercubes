Overview & Introduction

Welcome to Timbercubés, the premier destination for Modular Wardrobes and Interior product designing in Kerala. Your bedroom should be your ultimate personal sanctuary—a place of rest, relaxation, and perfect organization. However, a cluttered space with inadequate storage can quickly ruin that tranquility. That is where our bespoke wardrobe design services come in. Whether you need a sleek, space-saving sliding wardrobe for a compact apartment in Kochi, or a luxurious, expansive walk-in closet for a large home in Calicut, our expert interior designers are here to make your vision a reality.

We are recognized as the No.1 Modular Wardrobes Designers and Dealers in Thrissur, offering highly customizable storage solutions that do not compromise on aesthetics. Our wardrobes are meticulously engineered to maximize every square inch of your available space while seamlessly complementing your bedroom's interior architecture. From elegant swinging doors in deep wood grains to modern, reflective glass sliding panels, our extensive collection of designs, styles, and colors guarantees that you will find the perfect fit for your lifestyle and budget.

Key Benefits of Custom Wardrobes

Off-the-shelf furniture rarely meets the specific needs of a modern household. By investing in a custom wardrobe from Timbercubés, you unlock a multitude of benefits that drastically improve your daily routine and enhance the value of your home.

Aesthetics & Unmatched Personalization

A custom wardrobe is a blank canvas. You have complete control over the exterior finish, ensuring it perfectly matches the color palette and design theme of your bedroom. We offer an incredible range of materials including Acrylic, Poly Acrylic, Laminated, PVC foil, Lacquered Glass, and Solid wood. Whether you desire a minimalist, matte-white finish to create an airy atmosphere, or a sophisticated, dark walnut texture to add warmth and luxury, our designers will help you achieve a look that is exclusively yours.

Durability & Premium Marine Ply Construction

Quality is the cornerstone of everything we build at Timbercubés. All our interior furniture and fit-outs are constructed using premium Marine ply, ensuring exceptional strength, longevity, and high resistance to the humid climate typical of Kerala. The hardware we use—from the soft-close hinges on swinging doors to the heavy-duty tracking systems on sliding doors—is sourced from top-tier international brands, guaranteeing smooth, silent, and reliable operation for years to come without the hassle of frequent repairs.

Optimized Storage & Intelligent Compartmentalization

The true magic of a custom wardrobe lies behind its doors. During the design process, we analyze your specific storage needs. Do you need more hanging space for long dresses and suits? Do you require specialized pull-out drawers for accessories, jewelry, and ties? How about integrated shoe racks or secure digital safes? We design the internal layout to accommodate your exact inventory, ensuring that everything has a designated place. This intelligent compartmentalization completely eliminates clutter and makes getting ready in the morning an absolute joy.

Our Customization & Installation Process

Our functional yet stunning designs always keep the end-user in perspective. With branches and a well-equipped manufacturing unit serving Thrissur, Malappuram, Palakkad, Kozhikode, and Kochi, we ensure a seamless and stress-free experience from start to finish.

Consultation and 3D Modeling

The process begins with an in-depth consultation with our professional interior designers. We will visit your home to take precise measurements and discuss your storage requirements. We will then present you with advanced 3D models of your proposed wardrobe, allowing you to visualize exactly how it will look and function within your bedroom before any manufacturing begins.

Efficient Manufacturing and Timely Installation

All our products and services are delivered to exceed customer expectations in terms of time, quality, reliability, and cost. Your wardrobe is precision-cut and finished in our state-of-the-art factory. Once ready, our professional installation team will assemble the wardrobe in your home swiftly and cleanly, causing minimal disruption to your daily life.
