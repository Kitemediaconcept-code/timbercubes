Overview & Introduction

Welcome to the forefront of modern interior design with Timbercubés, the No.1 Modular Kitchens & Wardrobes Designers in Kerala. As the leading modular kitchen cabinet manufacturers and dealers in Thrissur, Calicut, Malappuram, Palakkad, and Kochi, we are incredibly proud to present our exclusive Lacquered Glass and Laminate Kitchens. This design philosophy marries the stunning, ultra-reflective brilliance of lacquered glass with the grounded, subtle, and tactile nature of premium laminates, creating a kitchen space that is both visually breathtaking and profoundly functional.

Lacquered glass, often referred to as back-painted glass, provides an unmatched level of luminous shine. When this pristine, glossy material is strategically contrasted with the matte or textured finish of high-quality laminates, it introduces a dynamic interplay of light and shadow into your home. This dual-material approach breaks the monotony of standard single-finish kitchens, offering a bespoke, high-end look that is favored by top interior designers across India. Whether you reside in a bustling Kochi apartment or a spacious villa in Thrissur, this kitchen style instantly elevates the entire architecture of your home.

Key Benefits of Lacquered Glass & Laminate Kitchens

Combining lacquered glass with laminate isn’t merely an aesthetic choice; it’s a highly strategic decision that maximizes the practical benefits of both premium materials, providing you with a kitchen that performs just as beautifully as it looks.

Aesthetics & Luminous Elegance

The primary draw of lacquered glass is its absolute flawlessness. The glass reflects natural and artificial light with incredible clarity, making the kitchen appear vastly more spacious and airy. By placing lacquered glass on upper wall cabinets and pairing them with earthy, textured laminates on the lower base units, we ground the space while keeping the eye drawn upwards. This contrast not only highlights the vibrant, fade-resistant colors of the painted glass but also emphasizes the sophisticated textures of the laminate, delivering an interior design that is visually balanced and deeply satisfying.

Durability & Unmatched Resilience

At Timbercubés, we utilize only the finest quality toughened glass and marine ply for our cabinet constructions. Toughened lacquered glass is exceptionally strong and highly resistant to shattering, making it perfectly safe for active family environments. Because the paint is applied to the back of the glass, the color is completely protected from scratches, heat, and moisture by the solid glass barrier in front of it. Paired with our heavy-duty, high-pressure laminates, this kitchen configuration offers incredible long-term durability that can easily withstand the heavy daily usage typical of a busy Kerala household.

Maintenance & Easy Cleaning

One of the most celebrated features of glass is how effortlessly easy it is to maintain. Grease, food splatters, and fingerprints cannot penetrate the non-porous glass surface. A simple wipe with a standard glass cleaner leaves the cabinets looking immaculately clean and brilliantly shiny in seconds. Similarly, our premium laminates are designed to be stain-resistant and incredibly easy to care for, ensuring that your dual-finish kitchen remains highly hygienic with minimal effort.
