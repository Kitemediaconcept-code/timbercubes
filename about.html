ABOUT US.

TimberCubes began in 2008 with one goal: build every kitchen and interior to the same dependable standard, planned around what each house actually needed instead of a repeated design.

That approach grew into a full interior business spanning 18 years and over 1000 completed projects across Kerala, built with the same craftsmanship at every stage. Justdial has recognised this consistency, rating TimberCubes 5 stars and naming it a Users' Choice award winner.

Abdul Jalal Panthenkaden, our Founder and CEO, has led the company since it started, bringing 18 years of experience in India and another 8 in the Middle East. We're based in Thrissur, where it all began and still home today.

Our Mission

Our mission is to design and build kitchens, wardrobes and interiors made specifically for each customer's space and requirements. Every project brings together practical design, precision manufacturing, quality materials and professional installation.

Our Vision

Our vision is to keep pace with how interior design, materials and manufacturing technology change over time. We carry that forward into every home and space we build, with the same consistency and integrity we started with back in 2008.

Our Approach

How We Design

01 Every layout is planned around the actual room: its dimensions, storage needs and how it's used day to day
02 No design gets reused from one house to the next
03 Modern design is balanced with everyday functionality
04 Materials, finishes and hardware are chosen to fit the customer's budget
05 Quality and affordability are planned together, from the start
