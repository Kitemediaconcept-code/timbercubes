Frequently
Asked Questions

How much do complete home interiors cost?

The cost depends on the size of the home, the rooms included, the materials, finishes and hardware chosen, and any extra work such as ceilings, panelling or painting. After a site visit, you receive a detailed quotation that lists every item, with clear inclusions and exclusions.

How long does a complete home interior project take?

Complete home interiors usually take 6 to 12 weeks or more from final design approval, depending on the size and complexity of the project. Smaller jobs that cover several items, such as a kitchen, wardrobes and a TV unit, typically take 4 to 8 weeks.

Can I do just one room instead of the full home?

Yes. Timbercubes takes on single items, such as one modular kitchen or wardrobe, as well as complete home interiors.

Does Timbercubes make its own furniture?

Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes, furniture and other interior units are made at Timbercubes’ own manufacturing facility in Kerala. Each unit is checked before dispatch, then installed on site by the Timbercubes team.

Do you take on office and commercial interior projects?

Yes. Timbercubes handles interiors for offices, shops, showrooms, clinics, restaurants and educational spaces, as well as work for builders, architects and interior designers.

Is 3D design free?

No. 3D visualisation is a chargeable service where applicable. The charges and terms are shared in advance, and they apply even if you decide not to go ahead with the project.

What warranty do you provide?

Warranty periods range from about 6 months to 25 years, depending on the materials, hardware and products used. The exact terms are shared with you based on what you choose.

Can I make changes after the design is approved?

Changes can be discussed, but changes after final approval may affect the quotation, production schedule and delivery date. Any such impact is explained to you before work continues.
