About Timbercubes Interiors

Timbercubes is a leading Modular kitchen designers and dealers in Thrissur Kerala, we also deal with Wardrobes and Kitchen Interior product designing and manufacturing. We are the best modular kitchen designers in Thrissur with a well-equipped manufacturing unit with professional interior designers to work on your dream kitchen cabinets. We offer our services in Kochi, Thrissur, Malappuram, Palakkad and also in Calicut. Our attractive, yet flexible designs keep in mind your space constraints to offer the added benefits of lower cost and utmost comfort. Our furniture and fit-outs comes with built in Acrylic, Poly Acrylic, Laminated, PVC foil, Lacquered Glass, PVC Foam sheet, Marine ply and Solid wood materials.
