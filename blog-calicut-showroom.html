Showroom October 26, 2017 • Calicut, Kerala

The Timbercubes Showroom Inauguration in Calicut: Pride, Gratitude and a New Beginning

26 October 2017 is a date we remember fondly at Timbercubes.

On that day, at 11:30 AM, we opened our new showroom in Calicut. From then on, customers could visit us, see our work and talk to us about their homes. It was a proud and memorable day for our team, and an important step in bringing quality kitchen, wardrobe and interior solutions to more customers.

In the Company of Respected Guests

The showroom was inaugurated by Faizal Nirmaan, Managing Director of Nirman Designs. Joining him were these distinguished guests:

  • Musthafa T. V., Chairman of Mother & Mahar Group
  • Pazheri Abdul Karim, Managing Director of Pazheri Builders & Developers
  • Ajeeb Komachi, renowned photographer

It was a joy to have them with us. Their presence made the day even more meaningful for our whole team, and it filled the occasion with happiness, pride and new beginnings.

More Than a New Space: Our Vision in Calicut

A showroom lets customers see the finishes, open the drawers and check the size of a wardrobe for themselves. For a team that makes kitchens, wardrobes and interiors, this hands-on view is very valuable.

For us, the Calicut showroom was more than a new space. It helped us reach more families and support them in creating homes that are beautiful, comfortable and practical.

Our vision has always been to bring together thoughtful designs, quality materials and functional interiors that suit the needs of each customer. The showroom stands for this vision, and for our wish to keep growing with the trust and support of our customers.

Thankful for Everyone Who Was There

We still remember that day with happiness. Thank you to our guests, well-wishers and everyone who joined us, for being a part of it.

With the same warmth and support, we look forward to many more good days.

Some milestones mark progress. Others become memories we carry with pride. The Calicut showroom inauguration was both.

Founder & Blog Author

Abdul Jalal Panthenkadan

Founder & Blog Author, Timbercubes (Est. 2008)

~20 Years in Interior Industry 8 Years UAE Business Experience NIPHM Certified in Pest Management

Abdul Jalal Panthenkadan is the founder of Timbercubes, established in 2008. He has nearly two decades of experience in the interior industry, and eight years of professional experience in Dubai, UAE, gave him a broader understanding of business.

His expertise covers modern interior concepts, kitchen and wardrobe manufacturing, furniture materials, hardware and emerging design trends. He has also completed a certification course in Rodent and Household Pest Management from the National Institute of Plant Health Management (NIPHM), Government of India, which adds to his knowledge of protecting interiors and materials.

Through his blogs and articles, he shares his industry knowledge, practical experience and views on the latest developments in interior design and manufacturing. His aim is to give readers useful ideas that help them understand interior spaces, materials and design possibilities.

He believes good interiors are not just about appearance. They should make spaces beautiful, functional and comfortable, and this belief guides his work at Timbercubes.
