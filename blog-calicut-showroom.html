Showroom August 15, 2023 • Calicut, Kerala

Calicut Display Showroom Grand Inauguration

Bringing World-Class Modular Living to Calicut

Timbercubes officially opened its flagship experiential display showroom in Calicut. Designed to offer homeowners an immersive touch-and-feel encounter, the showroom showcases our latest modular kitchen layouts, walk-in closets, and customized living room consoles.

Visitors can inspect authentic hardware mechanisms, test silent soft-close hinges, touch anti-scratch finishes, and consult directly with our in-house 3D interior design team.

What You Can Experience at the Showroom

  • Live Kitchen Models: Island, L-Shape, Parallel, and Straight kitchen models complete with built-in hob and chimney fittings.
  • Designer Wardrobe Suites: Floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobes, tinted glass finishes, and sensor-activated internal LED lighting.
  • Material Library: Browse over 200+ samples of high-gloss acrylic, PU coating, lacquered glass, and premium laminates.

We invite families, architects, and builders across Malabar to visit our Calicut showroom and take the first step in planning their dream home interior.
