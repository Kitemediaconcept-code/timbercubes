KITCHEN LAYOUTS

Find the Perfect Layout for Your Space

Your room’s size and shape decide which layout works best for a modular kitchen in Thrissur.

L-Shaped Kitchen

Fits small and medium kitchens along two connecting walls, with a magic corner unit storing pots and pans in the joining corner.

U-Shaped Kitchen

Suits larger kitchens where more than one person cooks, with counters on three sides and room for tall pantry units.

Straight Kitchen

Suits narrow kitchens and apartments, with tall units and lift-up cabinets adding storage upwards.

Parallel Kitchen

Suits long, narrow kitchens, keeping cooking on one side and washing on the other.

Island Kitchen

Suits large, open kitchens, adding a centre counter for cutting and preparation.

What does a modular kitchen cost?

There is no fixed price, because every kitchen is built to its own measurements. After the site visit, you get a quotation listing each material, finish and hardware item, with clear inclusions and exclusions.

How long does it take to make and install a modular kitchen?

About 3 to 5 weeks from final design approval to installation, depending on the materials chosen and site readiness. If you have a housewarming or move-in date, share it early, because the schedule is agreed before production starts.

Which finish suits Kerala’s humidity?

The core board behind the finish matters more than the finish itself. BWP 710 Gurjan marine plywood, WPC and PVC foam board are the moisture-resistant core options, and laminate, acrylic or PU can go over any of them.

What is the difference between laminate, acrylic and PU finishes?

Laminate is a printed sheet pressed onto the shutter, in solid colours and wood grains. Acrylic is a glossy sheet bonded to the shutter. PU is a sprayed paint coating with a seamless, high-gloss look. Your quotation shows the cost of each.

Can a modular kitchen be fitted in an existing kitchen during renovation?

Yes. Timbercubes takes on renovation projects as well as new homes. The site visit checks the walls, ceiling height, plumbing and electrical points so the new kitchen fits the existing space.

Can I choose my own brands for hardware and accessories?

Yes. Your preferred brands of hardware, fittings and accessories can be used where they are technically suitable and available. Brand choice affects the cost, and the quotation shows this.

What does the warranty cover?

Warranty periods range from 6 months to 25 years, depending on the materials, hardware and products chosen. The exact terms for your kitchen are shared before work begins.
